World's Best Lasanga

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 jar (about 500g) tomato pasta sauce
  • 9–12 lasagna sheets
  • 500ml béchamel/white sauce
  • 150–200g grated mozzarella or cheddar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  1. Cook the beef: Heat the oil in a large pan. Add the onion and cook for 3–4 minutes. Add the garlic and beef mince. Cook until the beef is browned.
  2. Add the tomato sauce: Stir in the pasta sauce. Season with salt and pepper. Simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  3. Layer the lasagna.
  4. Bake at 180°C fan / 200°C conventional for about 35–40 minutes.
  5. Leave it for 5–10 minutes before serving.
